RepBook Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

RepBook ("the app") is a workout journal for iPhone. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and how you can control or remove it.

Guest mode (default)

By default, RepBook works entirely offline. Your workouts, templates, gyms, and exercises are stored only on your device. We never see this data, and nothing is sent to us or anyone else unless you choose to sign in.

If you choose to sign in with Apple

Signing in is optional and lets you back up your workout history and restore it on another device. If you sign in, we collect and store:

  • Your email address (or Apple's private relay address if you choose to hide your real email) and display name, to identify your account.
  • Your workout history, templates, gyms, exercises, and related settings, so they can sync across your devices.

This data is stored with our backend provider, Supabase, which hosts the database on our behalf. We do not sell this data, and we do not use it for advertising. We do not use any third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Exporting or deleting your data

From Account → Privacy & Data inside the app, you can:

  • Export your data as a CSV or JSON file at any time.
  • Delete your account, which permanently removes your account and all associated data from our servers. This does not affect data stored only on your own device.

Children

RepBook is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a material way, we will update the date above.
